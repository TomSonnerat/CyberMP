CyberMP

A multiplayer mod for Cyberpunk 2077 game

Presentation

CyberMP is a multiplayer mod for Cyberpunk 2077, bringing the futuristic dystopian world of Night City to life in an exciting new way—through shared gameplay experiences with other players. It transforms the single-player RPG into a dynamic online experience, allowing players to explore, interact, and immerse themselves in the sprawling cyberpunk universe together.


CyberMP reinvents Night City as a shared digital universe, where the gritty cyberpunk setting becomes even more engaging. Whether you want to collaborate, compete, or simply socialize, CyberMP offers endless possibilities.
